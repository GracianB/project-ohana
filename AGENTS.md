# Ohana development

Kilo V1 is closed at ohana-445 / kilo-v1.0.0. Its accepted controls, strategy,
visual identity and room are specified in docs/KILO-V1.md. Do not redesign or
rebalance Kilo while delivering the remaining characters unless the user
explicitly reopens that scope. Necessary bug and compatibility fixes remain
allowed and must preserve the Kilo regression tests.

Keep hero-specific changes isolated inside shared combat/movement modules.
Closing Kilo does not imply that the full game's V1 or multiplayer is complete.

Apply the common delivery checklist in docs/CHARACTER-PHASES.md to each hero/room.
Kilo selection and Claro miniboss are finalized in phase 1, ohana-452.
